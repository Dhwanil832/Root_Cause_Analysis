## S1 — Notification

During preparation to isolate the number-one slab caster dryer-air system for planned maintenance, the RHOB service experienced a loss of instrument-air pressure. The crew had collected its work packet and isolation equipment following the pre-shift briefing. Troubleshooting followed; service pressure was restored later that morning. The work assignment was paused during the interruption.

This notification records the service interruption, not a diagnosis of the compressor, branch configuration or issued instruction. No injury or quantified production-loss assessment is supplied with this notification.
