# Utilities service directory

Document ID: IA-D01
Revision: 1
Record type: service and measurement directory
Origin: IC-1, Harbor Works
Effective: 2024-05-01
Scope: 1SC air system and RHOB service; orientation extract, not pipe topology

## S1 — Assets

1SC means the number-one slab caster area. D101 identifies its dryer. RHOB is the downstream process service receiving instrument air. H-U names the common utility supply header; H-R names the RHOB service header. Equipment availability and downstream service pressure are recorded separately.

## S2 — Measurement points

P-U senses pressure at H-U. P-R senses pressure at H-R. Values are bar gauge, not absolute pressure. A point's value does not establish the state of every pipe or valve between the points. This directory does not show the routes connecting H-U and H-R or the meaning of every alarm label.
