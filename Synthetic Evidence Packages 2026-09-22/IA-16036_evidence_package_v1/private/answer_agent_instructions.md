# Answering job — IA-16036

This instruction is for an isolated answering job, not the RCA investigator. Its purpose is to supply scoped information about two supply paths, series bypass valves, measurement/indication meaning and installed-versus-issued instruction status; it is not to finish the causal board.

The controller supplies actual questions from one model track, real question/merged-original IDs, specialist recipients, parent version, the approved release's original documents, previously public answers and only eligible answer-bank entries. Do not read the whole package or future inventory. The controller may use private eligibility data; do not disclose that a hidden future answer exists.

1. Match the information need, not exact question wording. Search the permitted originals even when no bank phrase matches.
2. Split composite questions into subparts. Use `answered`, `partial`, `conflicting`, `not_established` or `unavailable` at subpart level. A single location/state fact cannot also answer calibration, capacity, intent or applicability.
3. Cite document ID, exact revision and section/row plus a short verbatim excerpt. Source existence does not establish entailment. Distinguish observations, copied accounts and interpretations.
4. If a permitted correction supersedes a source, use its corrected scope. Preserve old wording only as attributed historical evidence and identify the correction. Do not treat the old interpretation as a second independent fact.
5. Preserve model-track ID, actual question ID, merged-original question IDs, recipients, parent version and still-unanswered purposes. Need IDs in the bank are private aids, never substitute runtime IDs.
6. Reuse a shared fact across merged requests without converting every subpart to answered. Route the same factual answer back to all relevant specialists via the broker.
7. For crew intent, why the change impact field was No, why the draft remained unissued, complete damage assessment and quantitative air-flow behavior, return the limits supported by permitted sources. Do not improvise a convenient log, testimony or motive. No web search can establish incident-specific facts.
8. Output a candidate batch with cited answers, subpart coverage, proposed permitted attachments and unresolved needs. **Stop.** Do not commit a new RCA version, trigger the next job, alter documents or contact the investigator outside the controller.
9. If two permitted sources conflict, preserve both with their scope and dates. A later creation date alone does not settle authority. If a source identity or release status is absent, flag it to the controller rather than inventing it.

No fixed number of questions is assumed. Useful partial coverage is preferable to fabricated completion. The full private answer bank and causal reference are never necessary input to this job.
