## S1 — Observations

The beam and link were on the sled/scaffold below the stand. The keeper was still installed and the link appeared intact. No damage was apparent on the yoke during this visual check. A small liner from the top operator-side window was found with the fallen assembly. No measurement here establishes keeper capacity or exact engagement before release.

OBS-1 and OBS-2 were walking beside the stand. They saw the assembly fall, but neither had a clear view of the hidden link/yoke contact immediately before movement. They did not report anyone in the pit at the time.

## S2 — Exposure and containment

Contractor personnel had worked in the pit during the morning. This does not establish continuous occupancy up to the fall. The area was red-flagged after the event. This record does not determine the order in which the liner and carrier first became free.
