# Roughing-area equipment directory

Document ID: R3-D01
Revision: 1
Record type: area orientation extract
Origin: MECH-1, Harbor Works
Effective: 2024-09-01
Scope: R3 stand, adjacent aisle and service pit; orientation, not a dimensional drawing

## S1 — Locations

The operator-side aisle is beside the stand. The service pit is below the carrier assembly. A sled and temporary scaffold may occupy the pit during outage work. A person beside the stand is not necessarily below the assembly.

## S2 — Terms and functions

| Asset term | Meaning in this area |
| --- | --- |
| Exit / delivery carrier beam | The same carrier assembly on the delivery side of R3 |
| Link | Connects the carrier assembly to its bearing interface at the yoke |
| Yoke | Interface carried by the top backup-roll balance mechanism |
| Keeper | Retaining feature at the link/yoke interface; its coverage must be read from the applicable assembly detail |
| Mill housing, spreader and posts | Stationary structures around the moving assembly |
| Backup rolls | Rolls normally installed for operation; removal changes the available space around the carrier |

This directory does not establish clearances, restraint capacities or the state of a particular outage assembly.
