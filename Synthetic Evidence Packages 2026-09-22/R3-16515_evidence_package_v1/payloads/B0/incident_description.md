# R3 outage notification

Document ID: R3-I00
Revision: 1
Record type: initial incident notification
Origin: SHIFT-1, Harbor Works roughing area
Created: 2024-10-14; exact entry time not recorded
Scope: R3 exit carrier assembly, Turn 2

## S1 — Notification

At approximately 13:03 on October 14, the R3 exit carrier beam and link fell from the yoke into the mill pit, landing on a sled and scaffold platform. Nobody was working in the pit at that moment; contractors had worked there during the morning. Two contractors beside the stand witnessed the fall. The area was red-flagged and work stopped.

The mill was in an outage configuration. Work rolls and backup rolls had been removed on the previous shift, and the top backup-roll balance had been left raised. An initial visual check found the link and keeper still assembled. The cause of release is under investigation.

## S2 — Time fields

The narrative uses approximately 13:03. The incident entry's structured time field reads 13:05. The difference has not been reconciled. Clock timezone is not recorded.
