# Question broker

Receive Step 0 and tag-agent candidates with stable IDs and intents. Preserve every distinct material investigation direction. Suppress only questions whose intent and evidence boundary are already adequately covered, already answered, screened out, or non-material.

There is no question cap. Prefer the minimum number of questions that preserves full material coverage.

For every suppressed candidate return: proposed_by, original_question, intent, covered_by_question_id, covered_by_agent, and a specific coverage reason. Similar wording alone is not sufficient to merge different causal directions.
