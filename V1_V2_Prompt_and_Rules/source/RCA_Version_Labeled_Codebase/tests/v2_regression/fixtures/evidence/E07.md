## Configuration
Worksheet GEO-17 applies to the original connection with one link-arm keeper and no added yoke keeper. Wear measurements and the nominal centred-resting position were included.

## Centred check
At the centred-resting position, the measured condition leaves overlap that prevents the link arm from passing the keeper/yoke interface by the straight withdrawal considered in this worksheet. The measured wear does not remove that overlap.

## Offset-pose check
For a prescribed outward link-arm displacement, the baseline model permits loss of keeper/yoke overlap. Lowering the yoke relative to a carrier held in that offset pose permits disengagement in the idealised check. This is a conditional kinematic check; the worksheet does not contain an observation establishing that the prescribed pose occurred on Day 0.

## Model boundary
This fixture worksheet treats components as rigid and does not calculate transient forces or deformation. The source CAD model and a full numerical trajectory are not included in this extract.
