# Change and work-record requirements

Record ID: IA-D03 | Revision: 4 | Effective: 2024-03-01

## Change records

Section 3.2: When a service-routing change affects an existing maintenance boundary, the change owner identifies the affected energy-control procedure (ECP), drawing and receiving operating area. Engineering disposition is required before the changed arrangement is used for that maintenance boundary.

Section 3.4: Mechanical completion verifies the installed work covered by the completion sheet. Work-document disposition and distribution are separate signoffs. A drawing redline is not, by itself, an issued operating document.

Section 3.6: The affected ECP is either revised and issued, or an approved temporary instruction is attached to the work package. Draft preparation alone does not satisfy distribution.

## Work records

Section 5.1: The work package records the ECP revision supplied to the crew and the maintenance boundary's acceptance state. An initial notification may be entered before the event sequence has been checked.

Section 5.3: Competence and authorization records define the tasks for which a person is qualified. They do not record what that person actually observed or did on a particular job.
