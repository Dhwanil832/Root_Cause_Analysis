# Fixed case and limits — evaluator only

Case version: IA-16036-1.0. Do not rewrite the case to reward a model's theory.

## Mechanism the reconstruction supports

H-U normally supplies H-R through M-101, D-101 and M-102. B-17 provides another route through **both** V-201 and V-202 in series. Opening V-201 alone does not create an open route to H-R when V-202 remains closed.

During preparation, V-201 was opened. M-101 was then closed while V-202 remained closed. M-102 was still open, but it did not restore a supply around the closed M-101. H-U pressure remained normal while H-R pressure decayed. V-202 was subsequently opened with M-101 still closed, and H-R pressure recovered. The connectivity, states and timing jointly support interruption of the receiving service by the unavailable normal route and incomplete auxiliary route.

The issued ECP revision 3 did not include the installed B-17/V-201/V-202 arrangement. Revision 4-D existed only as a draft. CH-218's “No” boundary-impact classification meant the workflow generated no ECP/distribution task. This establishes a specific document/configuration-control deficiency in the synthetic case. Why the classification was selected, why the draft remained unissued and what individuals knew remain unresolved. Do not require a fabricated motive to declare this evaluation useful.

## Evidence versus causal confidence

- Existence and content of records can be supported without every interpretation being proved.
- Series connectivity is directly stated by the B-17 segment records; its consequence requires linking it to observed valve positions.
- A documented close/open operation plus pressure response strongly supports the service-interruption mechanism. We do not have a calibrated flow model or proof excluding every transient contributor.
- The outdated issued ECP is supported. Its contribution to incomplete preparation is well supported by the actual supplied folder and interviews, but individual cognition is not observed.
- The routing mismatch is supported as a workflow fact. The ultimate organizational reason is unknown.
- “Source compressor tripped” is unsupported in B1 and contradicted by the retained trip journal and recorder correction in B3. This does not prove universal compressor perfection.
- No evidence supports deliberate misconduct, lack of authorization, an electrical arc, a pressure-vessel failure or a completed maintenance boundary.

## Allowed alternatives and unresolved matters

At B0, compressor interruption, distribution isolation, leakage, unusual demand and indication error are reasonable questions. At B1, stable upstream pressure and signal mapping weaken the compressor-trip story but do not identify the exact valve. At B2, the topology/state/recovery combination strongly discriminates the distribution explanation. At B3, the journal and document trail further constrain alternatives.

A model may propose an additional cause if it cites released evidence, states its uncertainty and explains a discriminating test. Do not mark a defensible alternative wrong merely because it is not the preferred phrasing. Conversely, matching the private cause before its evidence is released is a guess, not success.

No facts are supplied for employee intent, process damage, exact internal receiver volume, detailed demand, full legal compliance, economic loss, complete corporate change governance, or effectiveness of future corrective actions. Answers on these points must remain unavailable unless a separately versioned experiment adds evidence before the next run.
