# Question-to-source index

For the answer role only. This is a locator, not a required question list. Read the actual document before answering. Earliest batch is an access gate, not a cue to leak later details.

| Question family | Earliest batch | Records/sections to inspect | Boundary to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| What happened; where; what work was in progress? | B0 | IA-I01; IA-S01; IA-D01 | Preparation, not assumed completed maintenance isolation |
| What are the named areas/assets and who owns the records? | B0 | IA-D01 | Directory is not a piping diagram |
| What work documents did the crew receive? | B0 | IA-S02 attachment index | Actual supplied revision is recorded; adequacy not yet established |
| Were the assigned operators authorized? | B0 | IA-S03 | Qualification does not prove event-specific competence or conduct |
| What pressures changed and in which locations? | B1 | IA-P01 samples; IA-P02 point register | Join the point mapping to the measurements |
| Did the compressor trip; when did the alarm start? | B1 | IA-P01 alarm row; IA-P02 AL-44 mapping; IA-C01 basis | A screen label or acknowledgment is not a trip event or onset |
| What did the work document cover? | B1 | IA-P03 scope/index; IA-S02 | Missing identifiers alone do not establish applicability of another configuration |
| What connects the source to the receiver? | B2 | IA-P04 connection schedule | Read both B-17 segments, not just one valve name |
| What was actually open/closed and when? | B2 | IA-P05 worksheet/observations | Distinguish pre-restoration and later observations; preserve time uncertainty |
| What changed during recovery? | B2 | IA-P05 recovery row; IA-P01 samples; IA-P06 follow-up | Recovery association must be linked to topology, not just chronology |
| Did a different drawing apply? | B2 | IA-C02 applicability block; IA-P04; IA-S02 | Equipment identity, revision and issue state all matter |
| Was there leakage or a repair? | B2 | IA-P06 inspection scope | A limited walkdown does not prove universal absence of leakage |
| Was the installed change reviewed for the work boundary? | B3 | IA-P07 field values and routing rule; IA-D03 | Generated-task facts do not reveal motive |
| Was the revised procedure available to the crew? | B3 | IA-P08 issue/distribution table; IA-S02 | Draft, filed redline and issued crew copy are distinct |
| Can the early trip report be resolved? | B3 | IA-P09 journal/interview; IA-C01; IA-P02 | Correct the interpretation, not the fact that low pressure occurred |
| Why did someone select a field, ignore a step or act carelessly? | Any | No supplied record establishes these premises | Return not established; do not accept a question's allegation as a fact |

If several specialists asked the same thing, one evidence-backed answer can be returned with all recipient IDs. Preserve narrower unresolved subquestions even when the broad question was answered.
