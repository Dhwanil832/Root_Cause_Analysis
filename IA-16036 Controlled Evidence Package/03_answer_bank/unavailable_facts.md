# Facts not available in this package

No answer may be invented for:

- Personal intent, fatigue, disciplinary history, production pressure or an economic motive.
- The reasoning behind a change-form field selection.
- Full receiver volume, demand curve, leak-rate test or calibrated transient flow model.
- The internal condition of every valve at every instant.
- Complete plant electrical, process-control or safety-system design.
- Actual injury, damage or cost beyond what the case records expressly state.
- A complete legal-compliance determination.
- Whether a proposed future corrective action has already proved effective.
- Genuine original images, engineering drawings or signed records from the historical incident.

A text surrogate supplied in the synthetic package is not an actual photograph. Do not claim to have visually inspected an image that is not present. Lack of a record is a limitation, not automatic proof of an absent activity.
