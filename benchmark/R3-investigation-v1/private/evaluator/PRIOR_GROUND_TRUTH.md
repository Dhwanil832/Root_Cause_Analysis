## Canonical quantitative chain

At 100 cm2 effective area and 1:1 linkage it permits 4 cm = 40 mm displacement under the stated unloaded-by-block boundary. D08's 0.390-0.410 L interval corresponds to 39-41 mm, consistent with D07's 39-41 mm yoke movement. The later bench result 15-19 mL/min over 24 minutes corresponds to 36-45.6 mm, which is compatible but is not proof of the incident rate.

Relative withdrawal is yoke travel minus carrier travel. D07's bounded errors give [39,41] - [-1,1] = [38,42] mm. This exceeds the defined 30 mm initial primary seating engagement. The later fall at 13:24:03 remains an observed event; the correction does not prove that no later release motion happened. It invalidates the old threshold explanation rather than automatically establishing a replacement cause.
