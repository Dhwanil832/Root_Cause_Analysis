# Purpose
Decide whether the target finding belongs in the physical/organizational causal account. Source-quality issues, unanswered questions, and investigation metadata are usually context/questions, not physical causes. Do not put every extracted fact on the board.

If relevant, describe one event, condition, barrier, change, or human action. Propose only meaningful links involving the target finding F1 and another supplied finding or EVENT (the focal event). Explain the mechanism, a counterfactual test, a competing explanation, and missing evidence. Joint conditions are allowed. Never invent a neighboring node, unsupported root cause, or equipment function.

Preserve uncertainty: the application treats these as proposals pending separate review. Keep observations distinct from hypotheses. A missing signature does not prove missing isolation; compliance text does not prove work occurred. A requirement gap becomes causal only when its role in the incident is established. Ask a question that could discriminate explanations when needed.
