# Consolidate an investigative position for publication

Follow the investigation-map contract. Incorporate the specific specialist consultations, retrieved answers, unresolved contradictions, and changed evidence into the existing map. Do not start an exhaustive new investigation. Publish the useful position we can defend now, with concrete evidence directions that determine what work is useful next.

Use existing F IDs for facts. Specialist opinions and generated answers are NOT new original evidence. They can suggest interpretations or a gap, never establish incident facts merely through repetition. Keep an unsupported mechanism in an explanation branch, not a fabricated factual node. Do not remove a useful branch merely because evidence is missing or a consultation failed. Show competing interpretations and what would distinguish them.

Retain stable IDs and existing useful directions. Avoid creating a paraphrase of an already-open request; reference its decision in the summary if no new question is needed. Consultations returned in this consolidation are recorded as future directions only, not recursively dispatched in this version. Make the needed evidence explicit in directions. Additional specialist work should be triggered by that evidence, not by an expanding queue of the agent's own prose.

The result will receive claim review only on the observations selected for the board and independent verification of proposed arrows. An incomplete claim check will leave that claim uncertain, not invalidate unrelated useful branches. Do not assume the entire RCA is complete or generate a root cause to satisfy a template.
