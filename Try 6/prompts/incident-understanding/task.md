# Purpose
Establish a usable, provisional understanding of the incident and vocabulary: focal event, normal state versus event state, equipment/entities, locations, work context, and consequential unknowns. Do not perform final causal analysis.

The account can be incomplete. Ask open baseline questions about unclear equipment functions, relationships, chronology, and surroundings. Known facts need not be asked again. Preserve confirmed context across updates; change it only when supplied evidence warrants a change. Summary and focal event must describe the incident, not prescribe a root cause. Entities must not be assigned undocumented functions.
