# R3 ground-truth evaluation rubric — 100 points

Use this fixed rubric for each independent model run.

## 1. Incident comprehension — 10 points

- 2: Correct focal event, location, and affected equipment
- 2: Correct actual and potential human exposure
- 3: Recognizes roll removal, raised balance, and hydraulic shutdown as a changed outage configuration
- 3: Separates established facts from initial unknowns

## 2. Tag selection — 10 points

- 2: Equipment or mechanical involvement
- 2: Stored or released energy
- 2: Maintenance or outage work
- 2: Isolation or LOTO involvement
- 2: Relevant work-environment, planning, or human-exposure route without treating worker involvement as the cause

## 3. Investigative questions — 15 points

- 3: Link, yoke, keeper, stop, seating, and interference path
- 3: Hydraulic isolation, bleed-through, drift, and pressure-loss behavior
- 3: Gravity control, blocking, pinning, cribbing, and LOTO execution
- 2: Roll-removal configuration and approved sequence
- 2: Video, timing, and relative movement
- 2: Alternative causes, source conflicts, and evidence limitations

## 4. Evidence targeting — 15 points

- 3: Controlled drawings, design intent, geometry, and clearance calculation
- 3: Original video, annotated frames, or displacement timeline
- 3: Valve inspection, leak-down test, and hydraulic records
- 3: Task-specific LOTO plan, signed execution, and turnover records
- 3: Post-event component condition, measurements, and comparison evidence

## 5. Provenance and contradiction handling — 15 points

- 3: Distinguishes partner sources, controlled derivatives, synthetic facts, and open items
- 2: Correctly bounds the superseded R2 drawing
- 2: Correctly bounds the anonymous keeper note
- 2: Correctly bounds the crane rumor
- 3: Reconciles conflicts without silently selecting the convenient source
- 3: Carries raw-evidence limitations into its conclusions

## 6. Causal reasoning — 20 points

- 4: Identifies beam wedging against the spreader and posts
- 4: Identifies hydraulic bleed-through or pressure loss leading to yoke drift
- 4: Connects yoke drift plus stationary wedged beam to off-center link release
- 3: Identifies the missing stop or design prevention for the interference condition
- 3: Identifies inadequate gravity control and positive mechanical restraint
- 2: Identifies the limitation of the single-keeper retention arrangement

Full credit requires a connected sequence. Listing the terms without causal relationships earns no more than half of the available causal-reasoning points.

## 7. Corrective actions — 10 points

- 2: Engineered prevention of the interference or overtravel position
- 2: Positive mechanical restraint whenever the balance is raised
- 2: Replace and verify the R3 valve; test corresponding valves at other stands
- 1.5: Improve yoke, link, keeper, or tie-bar retention against the demonstrated path
- 1.5: Revise task-specific LOTO treatment of gravity and stored energy
- 1: Define inspection criteria and effectiveness verification

## 8. Calibration and trace quality — 5 points

- 2: Preserves uncertainty where raw evidence is absent
- 2: States alternatives and what evidence would discriminate them
- 1: Maintains a trace from question to evidence to claim to causal node and action

## Evidence-availability rule

Use the release ledger before scoring. If the model never received evidence needed for a finding, score the corresponding question and evidence request rather than penalizing it for failing to state the hidden answer. Record the finding as `not testable in this run`.
