# GTDE-06 — Measurement and component-condition summary

**Status:** Controlled benchmark derivative — not an actual company record  
**Basis:** Mechanical findings reported in the partner RCA and presentation

## Reported findings

- The R3 carrier beam weighed approximately 8,500 pounds.
- The carrier beam and link were recovered intact.
- The keeper remained installed.
- No apparent gross damage was reported on the yoke where the link normally rested.
- Wear was measured on the link-arm and yoke components.
- The reported measurements did not provide enough clearance for the link arm to dislodge from the yoke under the normal seated operating condition.

## Investigative interpretation

The reported condition weighs against a fracture, missing-keeper, or ordinary-clearance explanation acting alone. It supports testing an abnormal displacement or interference path.

## Evidence limitations

The benchmark does not include the raw measurements, measurement uncertainty, controlled drawing, tolerance stack, or clearance calculation. A model should retain those requests even if it identifies the abnormal-displacement mechanism.
