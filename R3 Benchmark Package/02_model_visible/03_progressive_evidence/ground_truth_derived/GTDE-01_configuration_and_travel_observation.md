# GTDE-01 — Configuration and travel observation

**Status:** Controlled benchmark derivative — not an actual company record  
**Basis:** Findings reported in the partner investigation presentation  
**Incident:** R3 carrier beam, October 14, 2024

## Recorded configuration

- The work rolls and the top and bottom backup rolls had been removed during the previous turn.
- The R3 top backup roll balance was isolated at the valve stand and left fully raised for clearance associated with the stripper guide box changeout.
- With the backup rolls removed, the top roll balance could occupy a raised position beyond the position normally limited by the installed-roll configuration.
- The B hydraulic system was later shut down and isolated for separate planned work after stripper guide box work had begun.

## Investigative significance

Roll removal changed the normal physical configuration and possible travel of the roll-balance carrier assembly. The record establishes the configuration change, but it does not by itself establish why the carrier beam separated from the yoke.

## Remaining checks

- Approved balance position for this exact outage configuration
- Required hold point after roll removal
- Required temporary or engineered restraint before hydraulic shutdown
- Actual time relationship between final roll removal, hydraulic shutdown, and observed movement
