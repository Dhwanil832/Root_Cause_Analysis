# GTDE-02 — Extended video review

**Status:** Controlled benchmark derivative — not an actual company record  
**Basis:** Video findings summarized in the partner investigation presentation  
**Original media supplied:** No

## Reported observations

- During the review interval, the delivery roll-balance carrier beam appeared hung in the mill-window housing.
- The upper part of the beam appeared wedged against the top mill spreader and mill posts.
- The yoke moved downward gradually over approximately two hours.
- The carrier beam did not move downward with the yoke while it remained wedged.
- Relative movement displaced the carrier beam and link arm away from the mill centerline.
- The link-arm stop eventually cleared the cylinder lifting point or yoke, after which the beam fell.
