# GTDE-05 — Task-specific LOTO review

**Status:** Controlled benchmark derivative — not an actual company record  
**Basis:** Energy-control findings reported in the partner RCA and presentation

## Task and isolation state

- The relevant task-specific procedure concerned the R3 stripper box changeout.
- The top backup roll balance had been left isolated in the raised position.
- The hydraulic system was shut down and bled for planned work.

## Review findings

- The investigation concluded that all relevant stored-energy sources were not fully accounted for during the task isolation.
- Gravity from the raised assembly remained a hazardous energy source after hydraulic shutdown.
- The reported field condition did not provide an adequate independent positive mechanical control for the raised assembly.
- The partner's follow-up required objects capable of drifting downward to be pinned, blocked, or cribbed as part of the LOTO procedure before personnel work on or below them.

## Interpretation boundary

This review identifies an energy-control gap. It does not, by itself, explain the complete physical separation of the carrier beam from the yoke. The physical mechanism still requires the configuration, interference, hydraulic, and retention evidence.
