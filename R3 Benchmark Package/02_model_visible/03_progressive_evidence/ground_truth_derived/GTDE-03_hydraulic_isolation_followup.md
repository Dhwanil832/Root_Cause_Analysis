# GTDE-03 — Hydraulic isolation follow-up

**Status:** Controlled benchmark derivative — not an actual company record  
**Basis:** Hydraulic findings reported in the partner RCA and presentation

## Reported findings

- The B hydraulic system had been shut down and isolated.
- The R3 hydraulic isolation valve was reported to have slow bleed-through.
- The investigation concluded that cylinder fluid could move back through the feed after system shutdown.
- Loss of hydraulic support was considered capable of allowing the yoke to drift downward under gravity.
- The organization replaced the R3 isolation valve and identified the corresponding valves at R1, R2, R4, and R5 for test and tryout.

## Maintenance-process observation

The partner causal chart states that isolation valves were not routinely inspected and were generally replaced when found defective.

## Evidence limitations

- No raw pressure historian data was supplied.
- No leak-down rate or elapsed-displacement calculation was supplied.
- No post-removal valve bench-test report was supplied.
- The record supports the reported investigation finding, but a forensic reviewer would still request the underlying test evidence.
