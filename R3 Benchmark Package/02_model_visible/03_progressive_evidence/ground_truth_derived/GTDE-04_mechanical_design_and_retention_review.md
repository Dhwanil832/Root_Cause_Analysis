# GTDE-04 — Mechanical design and retention review

**Status:** Controlled benchmark derivative — not an actual company record  
**Basis:** Design findings and actions reported in the partner RCA and presentation

## Reported design observations

- The original roughing-mill design incorporated one keeper on each link arm.
- The keeper remained installed on the recovered R3 link assembly.
- The arrangement did not include a carrier-beam stop that prevented the beam from becoming wedged on the mill spreader or mill posts when the backup rolls were absent.
- The investigation identified off-center displacement of the carrier beam and link arm as the path by which the link-arm stop could clear the yoke.

## Design questions raised by the investigation

- Should the system prevent the carrier beam from rising into an interference point?
- Should a stop retain the beam when backup rolls are removed?
- Should the keeper remain effective when the link is off center?
- Should the two link arms be tied together to limit asymmetric movement?
- Should the balance include an engineered bypass or safety stop?

## Limitation

The controlled assembly drawing, revision history, tolerance stack, and engineering calculations were not supplied. This record reports the partner investigation's design findings and the questions reflected in its corrective actions.
