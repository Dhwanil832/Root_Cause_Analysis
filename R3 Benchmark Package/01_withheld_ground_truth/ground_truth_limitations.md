# Ground-truth limitations and internal cautions

The partner package is the best available answer key, but it is not a complete forensic evidence archive.

## Limitations

1. The RCA PDF is Revision 0 and contains several nodes marked `Undefined`, question marks, or incomplete verification language.
2. The PowerPoint reports the hydraulic valve as “believed” to have slowly bled through. The underlying valve test, pressure history, and leak-down calculation were not supplied.
3. The presentation reports a video-based two-hour yoke drift and beam wedging, but the original video and annotated frame sequence were not supplied.
4. Component measurements and clearance conclusions are summarized, but the drawing, tolerance stack, measurement method, and raw sheet were not supplied.
5. The task-specific LOTO review is summarized, but the complete signed execution package was not supplied.
6. The causal chart refers to stored energy associated with “A” hydraulics in one location, while the incident narrative and presentation describe the B hydraulic system. Treat this as an answer-key inconsistency, not as a fact the blind model must discover unless an underlying source establishes it.
7. The RCA PDF shows action criteria as not checked, while the later presentation identifies several actions as completed. Preserve the time and revision distinction.

## Benchmark treatment

- Use the partner's accepted causal sequence as the scoring target.
- Do not describe every link as independently proven by raw evidence.
- Treat controlled derivative records as benchmark evidence, not authentic company records.
- Award calibrated uncertainty when the model identifies the reported mechanism but notes the missing raw evidence.
- Do not penalize a blind model for failing to detect contradictions that were never released to it.
