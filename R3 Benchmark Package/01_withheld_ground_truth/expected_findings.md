# Withheld expected findings

## Critical findings

A strong completed run should recover all of the following:

| ID | Expected finding | Minimum acceptable formulation |
|---|---|---|
| F-01 | Changed configuration | Backup rolls were removed and the balance was left raised, changing the normal support or travel condition. |
| F-02 | Wedging or interference | The carrier beam became wedged or hung against the top mill spreader and mill posts. |
| F-03 | Hydraulic drift | Slow bleed-through or pressure loss allowed the yoke to drift downward. |
| F-04 | Relative motion | The yoke moved downward while the wedged carrier beam remained in place. |
| F-05 | Link release path | Relative movement displaced the link off center until it cleared the yoke or lifting point. |
| F-06 | Missing design prevention | No stop or equivalent feature prevented the carrier beam from entering the interference position. |
| F-07 | Gravity-energy control | The raised assembly lacked adequate positive mechanical restraint and gravity was not fully accounted for in the isolation. |
| F-08 | Keeper limitation | The installed single-keeper arrangement did not prevent the identified off-center release path. |

## Secondary findings

- The beam weighed approximately 8,500 pounds.
- The beam and link were recovered intact and the keeper remained installed.
- No gross yoke damage was apparent.
- Measured wear did not explain release under the normal seated condition.
- No crane operation or direct worker interaction caused the fall at the event time.
- Contractors had occupied the pit earlier, creating serious potential exposure despite no injury.
- Isolation-valve inspection relied on finding defects rather than a routine inspection process.

## Alternatives the model should test and bound

| Alternative | Ground-truth treatment |
|---|---|
| Beam or link fracture | Not supported by the post-event condition reported in the RCA. |
| Keeper missing | Contradicted by recovery evidence. |
| Crane strike at event time | Not supported and contradicted by the reported absence of relevant crane lifts. |
| Worker contact | Not supported by the reported occupancy and witness information. |
| Wear or excess clearance alone | Not supported; reported measurements were insufficient to permit normal dislodgement. |
| Window liner initiated the fall | Not established. Keep open only if new evidence supports it. |

## Expected causal maturity

- **Initial:** identifies hazards, changed conditions, and questions.
- **Developing:** proposes wedging, drift, and retention hypotheses but leaves evidence gaps.
- **Supported:** connects the reported video, configuration, valve condition, measurements, and LOTO review into the accepted sequence.
- **Verified for benchmark purposes:** reconstructs the critical chain, identifies the evidence supporting each link, tests plausible alternatives, and keeps the limitations of the reconstructed records visible.
