# Withheld corrective-action alignment

The partner material reports the following actions. A model earns credit when it proposes the control purpose, even if its wording differs.

| Causal target | Partner-aligned action | Expected verification concept |
|---|---|---|
| Beam can rise or wedge at interference points | Engineer carrier-beam stops or system changes that prevent the beam from reaching the interference position | Demonstrated clearance and travel analysis across outage configurations |
| Yoke/link can separate when displaced | Upgrade the yoke/link connection and keeper arrangement; add a link-arm tie bar where engineering confirms | Engineering acceptance plus controlled fit-up and movement test |
| Raised assembly depends on hydraulic state | Prohibit leaving the top roll balance isolated upright unless it is pinned, blocked, or cribbed | Field observation and LOTO audit showing positive mechanical restraint |
| R3 isolation valve bleeds through | Replace the R3 hydraulic isolation valve | Post-installation isolation and leak-down verification |
| Similar valves may share the condition | Test R1, R2, R4, and R5 isolation valves and replace defective units | Recorded test criteria, results, and disposition for every stand |
| Gravity/stored energy omitted from task isolation | Update and communicate LOTO requirements to include all stored energy, including gravity | Revised task procedure, worker understanding check, and field verification |
| No routine condition-monitoring process | Establish a work-order inspection process for the roll-balance carrier system | Defined inspection frequency, acceptance criteria, completed records, and trend review |

## Action-scoring guardrails

- Do not reward generic retraining unless it directly addresses a verified procedural or knowledge gap.
- Do not accept “replace the keeper” as a complete action unless the proposed design controls the demonstrated release path.
- Do not accept hydraulic repair alone because it does not control gravity independently.
- Do not accept blocking alone as the only action because the design allowed wedging and the isolation valve condition also requires correction.
