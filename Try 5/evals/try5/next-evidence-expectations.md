# Next-evidence decision — frozen expectations

This is a single diagnostic of whether Qwen can use its previously proposed
pressure contrast to select a useful next evidence request. It is not a new
incident version, a root-cause judgment, a global one-question cap, or a retry of
the full investigation.

Use the saved Qwen digest and native thinking/sampling profile from the preceding
pilot, its eight original B0+IA-P01 passages, the three existing findings, and its
first proposed observation. Do not supply its failed explanation list, our source
audit, later documents, hidden truth, or this expectation file to the model.
The task is generically phrased; it does not name the desired document or physical
mechanism. The selected observation is supplied as a proposal, not established fact.

## Acceptance checks (all required for a clean pass)

1. Ground the contrast in selected P-U/P-R samples, preserving the distinction
   between recorded measurements and physical reality throughout the interval.
2. Ask a bounded, actionable evidence question tied to interpreting that contrast,
   rather than “what caused the incident?” or a broad procedural checklist.
3. Identify a missing comparison premise: current point identity/connection and
   operating arrangement, rather than treating the location directory as a current
   piping diagram. A request establishing these prerequisites may include the
   point register/current arrangement and validation of the records.
4. Handle reliability and timing/coverage as prerequisites or explicitly unresolved
   conditions. Good acquisition quality, steady sampled P-U and C-11 RUN are not
   proof of accurate continuous pressure, delivered flow or compressor health.
5. Explain at least two materially different possible answers and their warranted
   effects. If mapping/connection and valid comparable readings are confirmed, a
   between-point/path investigation becomes more defensible; a mapping or validity
   problem weakens that inference. Neither establishes a specific valve, worker
   error, design defect or final root cause. A valid alternative request can pass
   if it establishes another genuinely prior premise without bypassing these ones.
6. No unsupported causal/blame implications, invented document contents, false
   document identity, or relabeling unknown information as refutation.

Grade each criterion as pass/partial/fail with source-based explanation after the
response. API/schema success is separate. Do not repair the returned text or
change these criteria after seeing the response. A narrow success is not a claim
of generalization, whole-RCA acceptance, or passage of the 24-case claim-review gate.
