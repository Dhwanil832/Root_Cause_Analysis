# Rail / Locomotive Involvement

## Purpose

Explore rail movement, track and rolling-stock condition, switching, coupling, clearance, signaling, and exposure.

## Activate this tag when

- A locomotive, rail car, trackmobile, rail track, switch, derail, crossing, or rail movement is involved.
- Coupling, uncoupling, loading, unloading, car securement, switching, or pedestrian exposure near rail may matter.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What locomotive, rail car, track, switch, derail, or related equipment was involved?
2. What movement or rail task was occurring, and what was the intended route?
3. What were the positions and securement states of the rolling stock before the event?
4. What was the condition of the track, switches, derails, brakes, couplers, and warning systems?
5. Who controlled, authorized, directed, or observed the movement?
6. What signals, radio calls, confirmations, or switching instructions were used?
7. What clearance, fouling, crossing, pedestrian, vehicle, or loading conditions existed?
8. What changed from the planned consist, route, load, switching sequence, or operating condition?

## Branches and handoffs

- Communication or confirmation breakdown: activate Communication / supervision involvement.
- Track, rolling-stock, brake, or coupler condition: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Person positioning, qualification, or action: activate Human involvement.
- Route, switching plan, securement standard, or loading sequence: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Congestion, visibility, weather, or surface condition: activate Work-environment involvement.
- Moving, rolling, or released energy: activate Stored, released, or gravity energy.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the consist, route, movement sequence, control authority, signals, track condition, and securement facts. Ask only about disputed movement authority, unexplained equipment state, missing communications, newly identified rolling stock, or evidence that could alter the sequence.

## Expected result

Record the rail configuration and movement sequence, control and communication state, equipment and track conditions, exposure points, open causal paths, and handoffs.
