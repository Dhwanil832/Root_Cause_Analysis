# Communication / Supervision Involvement

## Purpose

Explore how information, authority, coordination, direction, feedback, and escalation moved among the people and groups involved.

## Activate this tag when

- Shift handover, radio, hand signals, instructions, warnings, confirmations, work authorization, or supervision may matter.
- Multiple crews, departments, contractors, operators, maintainers, or control points interacted.
- A concern, change, abnormal condition, or stop-work decision was or should have been communicated.

## First-pass branching questions

1. Who needed to communicate or coordinate, and for what decision or action?
2. What information was sent, received, understood, confirmed, or missing?
3. What communication method was used, and was it suitable for the conditions?
4. Who had authority and responsibility for the work, movement, isolation, or area?
5. What shift, contractor, department, or crew handoffs occurred?
6. What supervision, verification, or field presence was expected and available?
7. Were concerns, changes, conflicts, or abnormal conditions raised and resolved?
8. What records or independent evidence establish the communication sequence?

## Branches and handoffs

- Training, comprehension, language, or action concern: activate Human involvement.
- Missing briefing, signoff, plan, or authority definition: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Shift or contractor transfer of isolation: activate Isolation / LOTO involvement.
- Crane signals, rail movement authority, or mobile-equipment spotter: activate the corresponding operational tag.
- Outage or multi-crew coordination: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse identified parties, roles, messages, channels, authority, and handoff timeline. Ask only about disputed content, missing recipients, unverified acknowledgments, newly identified interfaces, or evidence that could change who knew what and when.

Avoid asking every participant the same broad question when records or earlier interviews already establish the communication.

## Expected result

Record the required and actual information flow, authority and handoffs, communication gaps or successful controls, evidence confidence, and handoffs.
