# Work-Environment Involvement

## Purpose

Explore whether the physical surroundings affected exposure, visibility, access, footing, atmosphere, equipment behavior, or the ability to perform work safely.

## Activate this tag when

- Height, ladders, scaffolds, platforms, openings, stairs, or access routes are involved.
- Walking surfaces, housekeeping, congestion, clearance, lighting, weather, noise, temperature, or visibility may matter.
- Dust, fumes, gases, oxygen deficiency, asbestos, ventilation, or another atmospheric condition may matter.
- The physical arrangement of the work area may have shaped the event.

## First-pass branching questions

1. Where did the event occur, and what physical features of the area were relevant?
2. Were height, access, openings, ladders, scaffolds, stairs, or changing elevations involved?
3. What were the walking and working surfaces, housekeeping, drainage, and congestion conditions?
4. What lighting, visibility, noise, weather, temperature, or wind conditions existed?
5. Was dust, fume, gas, oxygen level, ventilation, or another atmospheric exposure possible?
6. What clearances, barriers, exclusion zones, escape paths, and lines of sight existed?
7. Was the area in its normal condition, or had work, deterioration, or temporary arrangements changed it?
8. What photographs, measurements, monitoring results, inspections, or witness observations establish the conditions?

## Branches and handoffs

- Person positioning, PPE, or physical capability: activate Human involvement.
- Platform, scaffold, ladder, barrier, surface, or structural failure: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Work-at-height or atmospheric procedure and permit: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Temporary conditions from maintenance or outage: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.
- Process-generated heat, fumes, gas, dust, or material: activate Process or material involvement.
- Animal, wildlife, or insect encounter: activate Animal / wildlife / insect involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the location, layout, measurements, photographs, weather, monitoring, access, and surface findings. Ask only about changed conditions, missing measurements, conflicting observations, a newly relevant area, or environmental evidence that could change the causal path.

## Expected result

Record the environmental conditions that were relevant, those screened out, the effect on people/equipment/process, open evidence gaps, and handoffs.
