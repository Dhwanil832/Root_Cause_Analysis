# Crane, Lifting, or Suspended-Load Involvement

## Purpose

Explore lifting systems, load control, rigging, suspension, travel, landing, and exposure beneath or near loads.

## Activate this tag when

- A bridge, EOT, gantry, jib, monorail, mobile, or boom crane is involved.
- A hoist, chainfall, come-along, sling, magnet, lifting beam, hook, tong, or other lifting device is involved.
- A load was lifted, suspended, transferred, landed, released, dropped, or capable of falling.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What lifting equipment, rigging, attachment, and load were involved?
2. What stage of the lift or suspended condition existed at the time?
3. What were the load's weight, center of gravity, geometry, stability, and attachment points?
4. Were the crane, hoist, rigging, and lifting devices suitable and within rated capacity?
5. What inspections, condition checks, and lift planning applied?
6. Who operated, rigged, signaled, directed, or worked near the lift?
7. What line-of-fire, exclusion-zone, clearance, swing, or travel conditions existed?
8. What evidence explains any unintended movement, release, contact, shift, or fall?

## Branches and handoffs

- Falling load, gravity, tension, or stored mechanical energy: activate Stored, released, or gravity energy.
- Defective rigging, hoist, attachment, or crane component: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Operator, rigger, signaler, qualification, or positioning issue: activate Human involvement.
- Lift plan, capacity determination, inspection, or standard issue: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Signals, radio, handoff, or multiple-party coordination: activate Communication / supervision involvement.
- Congestion, wind, visibility, surface, or access concern: activate Work-environment involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the lift equipment, load, rigging, capacity, personnel roles, lift stage, and exposure-zone facts. Ask only about unresolved attachment or stability conditions, disputed signals or movement, missing inspection or capacity evidence, or newly identified equipment.

## Expected result

Record the lift configuration and sequence, load and device suitability, exposure controls, plausible movement or release paths, evidence gaps, and handoffs.
