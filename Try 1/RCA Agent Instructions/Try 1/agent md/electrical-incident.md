# Electrical Incident

## Purpose

Explore the electrical side of an incident far enough to identify credible electrical causal directions and appropriate handoffs. Do not treat the presence of electrical equipment as proof that electricity caused the event.

## Activate this tag when

- Electrical equipment, panels, circuits, conductors, wiring, controls, batteries, chargers, or temporary power are involved.
- Shock, arc flash, arcing, electrical heat, a trip, a blown protective device, or an unexpected electrical state is reported.
- Work may have occurred on or near energized electrical parts.
- An electrical malfunction may have changed equipment behavior.

## Do not activate solely because

- The facility or machine normally uses electricity.
- Electrical equipment is nearby but has no plausible connection to the event.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What electrical asset, circuit, or system could be involved?
2. What was the confirmed energy state immediately before and during the event?
3. What work, interaction, or change was occurring around the electrical system?
4. What evidence suggests an electrical event or abnormal condition?
5. What was different from the normal equipment or work condition?
6. What protection or control was expected to prevent exposure, and what was its observed state?
7. Who or what could have been exposed, and by what possible path?
8. Were surrounding conditions relevant to the electrical exposure or failure?

Possible evidence includes equipment condition, alarms, relay or trip records, meter readings, photographs, damage patterns, drawings, work orders, switching records, and witness observations.

## Branches and handoffs

- Uncertain hazardous-energy isolation: activate Isolation / LOTO involvement.
- Repair, testing, troubleshooting, outage, or temporary configuration: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.
- Damaged or malfunctioning electrical equipment: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Person-to-energy interaction, qualification, or PPE concern: activate Human involvement.
- Water, conductive surfaces, congestion, lighting, or access concern: activate Work-environment involvement.
- Missing or unsuitable procedure, permit, or job plan: activate Procedure / planning involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the identified asset, circuit, energy state, event evidence, and exposure path. Ask only about:

- a newly identified electrical component or circuit;
- conflicting evidence about energized or de-energized state;
- a new abnormal condition or equipment change;
- an unresolved electrical protection or exposure path;
- evidence that could materially change whether electricity contributed.

Do not repeat general questions about panels, wiring, batteries, arc flash, or energized work when the record has already answered them.

## Expected result

Record the electrical system involved, confirmed facts, unresolved electrical conditions, plausible electrical causal paths, evidence needed, and any activated tag handoffs.
