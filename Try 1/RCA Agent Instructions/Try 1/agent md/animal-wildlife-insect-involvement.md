# Animal / Wildlife / Insect Involvement

## Purpose

Explore animal, wildlife, insect, nest, bite, sting, contamination, or unexpected biological encounters that affected the work.

## Activate this tag when

- An animal, bird, rodent, reptile, insect, nest, hive, bite, or sting is involved.
- Animal presence affected access, equipment, visibility, movement, or worker response.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What animal, wildlife, or insect was involved, and how was it identified?
2. Where and when was it encountered, and was the presence expected or previously reported?
3. What contact, bite, sting, startle, distraction, contamination, or movement occurred?
4. Were nests, food sources, openings, seasonal conditions, or habitat factors present?
5. What immediate medical, first-aid, containment, or area-control response occurred?
6. What pest-management, inspection, warning, or reporting controls existed?
7. Did the encounter create a secondary exposure such as a fall, vehicle movement, dropped object, or equipment contact?
8. What evidence establishes the encounter and its role in the event?

## Branches and handoffs

- Area condition, access, weather, nesting, or habitat: activate Work-environment involvement.
- Person reaction, allergy, PPE, or medical exposure: activate Human involvement.
- Secondary equipment, vehicle, lifting, or energy event: activate the corresponding tag.
- Missing inspection, response plan, or warning: activate Procedure / planning involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the species or encounter description, location, contact, response, and site-condition facts. Ask only about uncertain identification, prior reports, unresolved medical or secondary exposure, or evidence needed to confirm material relevance.

## Expected result

Record the encounter, exposure mechanism, site contributors, response, secondary event path, evidence needs, and handoffs.
