# Equipment or Tool Involvement

## Purpose

Explore whether the design, selection, condition, setup, operation, or failure of equipment or tools contributed to the event.

## Activate this tag when

- Machinery, mill equipment, guards, fixtures, components, hand tools, power tools, or temporary equipment are involved.
- Equipment moved, failed, broke, jammed, drifted, released, contacted, or behaved unexpectedly.
- Tool suitability, compatibility, guarding, inspection, maintenance, or modification may matter.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What equipment, tool, component, or interface was involved?
2. What was it expected to do, and what did it actually do?
3. What was its physical and operating condition before and after the event?
4. Was it the correct equipment or tool for the task and conditions?
5. Had it been inspected, maintained, repaired, modified, or reported defective?
6. What setup, alignment, guarding, support, clearance, or attachment condition existed?
7. What operating limits, loads, tolerances, or failure modes may be relevant?
8. What physical evidence could distinguish failure, misuse, unsuitable design, or external influence?

## Branches and handoffs

- Energy release, pressure, raised component, or falling object: activate Stored, released, or gravity energy.
- Repair, change, or abnormal configuration: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.
- Electrical component or control fault: activate Electrical incident.
- Isolation required for examination or work: activate Isolation / LOTO involvement.
- Tool selection or operating instruction concern: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Person-equipment interface concern: activate Human involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the asset identity, normal function, observed condition, maintenance history, and preserved evidence. Ask only about new components, conflicting condition evidence, unresolved failure modes, or tests that can distinguish remaining hypotheses.

Do not repeat generic condition or maintenance questions after reliable inspection and records have answered them.

## Expected result

Record the equipment's expected and actual behavior, relevant condition and history, credible failure or interaction paths, required evidence, and tag handoffs.
