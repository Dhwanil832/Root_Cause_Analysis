# Mobile Equipment / Vehicle Involvement

## Purpose

Explore movement, control, visibility, route, condition, and person-equipment interaction involving mobile equipment or vehicles.

## Activate this tag when

- A forklift, loader, mobile material handler, truck, trailer, tractor, cart, buggy, or similar vehicle is involved.
- Vehicle travel, backing, parking, loading, unloading, stability, collision, or pedestrian exposure may matter.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What mobile equipment or vehicle was involved, and what task was it performing?
2. What was its movement, position, speed, load, and direction immediately before the event?
3. What route, travel surface, clearance, traffic pattern, and exclusion zone existed?
4. What could the operator and nearby people see or hear?
5. What was the equipment's condition, including brakes, steering, tires, alarms, lights, restraints, and attachments?
6. Was the load stable, secured, within capacity, and compatible with the equipment?
7. Who controlled or directed the movement, and how was coordination handled?
8. What changed from the normal route, task, equipment, load, or surrounding activity?

## Branches and handoffs

- Pedestrian behavior, operator capability, or PPE: activate Human involvement.
- Congestion, poor surface, lighting, weather, or visibility: activate Work-environment involvement.
- Mechanical condition or attachment issue: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Load lifting or suspension: activate Crane, lifting, or suspended-load involvement when appropriate.
- Traffic plan, spotter requirement, or task setup issue: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Signals or coordination issue: activate Communication / supervision involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the identified vehicle, route, load, movement, operator, visibility, and condition findings. Ask only about unresolved movement conflicts, newly identified people or equipment, disputed visibility, unverified equipment condition, or evidence that changes the travel sequence.

## Expected result

Record the movement sequence, equipment and load state, visibility and route conditions, interaction points, open causal paths, evidence needs, and handoffs.
