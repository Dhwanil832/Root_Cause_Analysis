# Isolation / LOTO Involvement

## Purpose

Explore the control of hazardous energy before, during, and after work. LOTO means lockout/tagout and includes isolating energy, applying personal or system controls, verifying the isolated state, controlling stored energy, and safely restoring energy.

## Activate this tag when

- Work required equipment or a system to be de-energized, isolated, blocked, or made safe.
- A lock, tag, tryout, disconnect, valve, key, group lockbox, or energy-restoration step is mentioned.
- The actual energy state is uncertain or differs from the expected state.
- Stored energy, multiple energy sources, temporary energization, or re-energization may matter.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What equipment or system was being isolated, and for what work?
2. What hazardous-energy sources and boundaries were identified?
3. What isolation points, locks, tags, blocks, blanks, disconnects, or valves were used?
4. How was zero energy or the intended safe state verified?
5. What stored, residual, induced, gravity, or secondary energy remained possible?
6. Who established, controlled, changed, or relied on the isolation?
7. Were group, contractor, shift-change, testing, temporary-energization, or restoration conditions involved?
8. What evidence confirms the isolation state at each important point in the event sequence?

## Branches and handoffs

- Electrical energy or electrical verification: activate Electrical incident.
- Pressure, gravity, hydraulic, pneumatic, or mechanical energy: activate Stored, released, or gravity energy.
- Outage, repair, troubleshooting, testing, or startup: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.
- Missing or unclear isolation procedure or boundary: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Shift handoff, group lockbox, contractor, or authority issue: activate Communication / supervision involvement.
- Qualification, understanding, or action concern: activate Human involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the equipment boundary, energy-source list, isolation points, responsible people, verification method, and restoration sequence. Ask only about:

- an unaccounted energy source;
- a conflicting isolation or verification record;
- a new person, shift, contractor, or lockbox interface;
- temporary energization or changed boundaries;
- evidence needed to confirm the actual state.

Do not repeat the entire LOTO procedure once the applicable steps and evidence are established.

## Expected result

Record the intended and actual isolation states, all relevant energy sources, boundary and verification evidence, transfer or restoration points, unresolved gaps, and handoffs.
