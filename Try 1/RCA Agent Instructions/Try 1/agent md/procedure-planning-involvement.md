# Procedure / Planning Involvement

## Purpose

Explore whether work was adequately defined, assessed, sequenced, resourced, and adapted to actual conditions.

## Activate this tag when

- A procedure, job plan, JSHA, risk assessment, permit, checklist, standard, or work instruction applies.
- Scope, sequence, staffing, equipment selection, timing, change management, or contingency planning may matter.
- The work differed from what was expected or documented.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What work was intended, and what governing procedure, plan, permit, or assessment applied?
2. Did the document match the actual task, equipment, hazards, people, and conditions?
3. What critical steps, limits, hold points, prerequisites, and contingencies were defined?
4. What resources, staffing, time, tools, and information were planned and available?
5. What changes or discoveries occurred, and how were they assessed and authorized?
6. Was the sequence workable, understandable, and consistent with how the job was actually performed?
7. What known hazards and previous lessons were incorporated?
8. What evidence shows how the plan was briefed, used, revised, or departed from?

## Branches and handoffs

- Person understanding, qualification, or work practice: activate Human involvement.
- Briefing, handoff, authorization, or coordination: activate Communication / supervision involvement.
- Outage, maintenance, or changed configuration: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.
- Energy-control plan: activate Isolation / LOTO involvement.
- Equipment or tool selection: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Environmental or process assumptions: activate Work-environment involvement or Process or material involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the governing documents, planned scope, hazard assessment, resources, sequence, and identified changes. Ask only about missing versions, actual use, unresolved deviations, undocumented changes, or whether a gap could materially affect the event.

Do not ask whether a procedure existed after the applicable document has been found; evaluate its relevance and use instead.

## Expected result

Record the intended work system, its fit to actual conditions, material planning or procedure gaps, changes and authorizations, supporting evidence, and handoffs.
