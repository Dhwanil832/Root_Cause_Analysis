# Maintenance, Outage, or Non-Routine Work

## Purpose

Explore how repair, inspection, cleaning, adjustment, changeover, outage, startup, shutdown, or unusual work changed normal equipment, process, roles, or controls.

## Activate this tag when

- Routine or non-routine maintenance is occurring.
- Equipment is removed, opened, bypassed, blocked, temporarily supported, or left in an unusual configuration.
- An outage, startup, shutdown, troubleshooting, commissioning, testing, or return-to-service activity is involved.
- Work scope or site conditions changed as the job progressed.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What work was planned, and what was actually being performed?
2. What equipment, process, or area was placed in a non-normal condition?
3. What had been removed, disabled, bypassed, opened, supported, or temporarily configured?
4. What work occurred on previous shifts or by other crews and contractors?
5. What scope changes, discoveries, delays, or conflicting activities occurred?
6. What condition was the system expected to be left in between work stages?
7. What inspections, hold points, testing, and return-to-service steps applied?
8. What evidence documents the configuration and work sequence?

## Branches and handoffs

- Hazardous-energy control: activate Isolation / LOTO involvement.
- Equipment condition, temporary support, or mechanical failure: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Missing scope, sequencing, permit, or change control: activate Procedure / planning involvement.
- Shift handoff, contractor coordination, or work ownership: activate Communication / supervision involvement.
- Human capability or task familiarity: activate Human involvement.
- Process material or environmental change: activate Process or material involvement or Work-environment involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the scope, work packages, configuration changes, shift sequence, responsible groups, and system state. Ask only about undocumented work, changed scope, conflicting timelines, newly identified temporary conditions, or missing turnover and return-to-service evidence.

## Expected result

Record the planned and actual work scope, configuration timeline, interfaces, changes, unresolved work-control gaps, evidence needs, and handoffs.
