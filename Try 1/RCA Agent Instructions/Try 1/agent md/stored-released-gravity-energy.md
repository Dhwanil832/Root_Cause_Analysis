# Stored, Released, or Gravity Energy

## Purpose

Explore hazardous energy that was stored, supported, pressurized, tensioned, elevated, released, or capable of causing movement.

## Activate this tag when

- An object fell, shifted, rolled, collapsed, swung, or moved under gravity.
- Hydraulic, pneumatic, steam, compressed-air, spring, tension, pressure, thermal, or mechanical energy may be involved.
- A raised, suspended, blocked, restrained, or pressurized condition existed.

## First-pass branching questions

1. What form or forms of energy were present?
2. Where was the energy stored, supported, restrained, or generated?
3. What prevented the energy from being released during normal conditions?
4. What changed immediately before the movement or release?
5. What object, fluid, component, or person was within the potential energy path?
6. What blocking, support, restraint, pressure control, or secondary protection existed?
7. Was the amount or direction of energy understood and accounted for in the work?
8. What physical evidence could identify the release point, sequence, and failure of containment or support?

## Branches and handoffs

- Required hazardous-energy isolation: activate Isolation / LOTO involvement.
- Failed support, restraint, valve, line, component, or structure: activate Equipment or tool involvement.
- Elevated or suspended load: activate Crane, lifting, or suspended-load involvement if lifting equipment is relevant.
- Work during repair, outage, setup, or temporary configuration: activate Maintenance, outage, or non-routine work.
- Exposure positioning or work practice: activate Human involvement.
- Missing energy assessment or control method: activate Procedure / planning involvement.

## If this tag was processed before

Reuse the energy type, source, magnitude when known, restraint, release path, and exposed zone. Ask only about unresolved energy sources, conflicting state evidence, newly identified restraints, or evidence required to reconstruct the release sequence.

## Expected result

Record each relevant energy source, its normal control, actual state, release or movement path, exposed targets, remaining hypotheses, and handoffs.
